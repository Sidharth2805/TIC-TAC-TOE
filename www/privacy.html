Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

Tic-Tac-Toe Machine is a client-side gaming application. We respect user privacy and adhere to data protection standards. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

2. Data Collection and Usage

This application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. All game actions, settings, custom player names, and statistics execute entirely on your device.

  • Local Storage: Gameplay statistics (wins, losses, draws) and preference configurations (theme, audio, player names) are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage API.
  • No Server Logs: No gameplay data, IP addresses, or device identifiers are recorded on external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The application operates autonomously. It does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social tracking pixels.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since data is saved strictly on your local browser, you retain complete control over it. You can erase all stored gameplay statistics and custom settings at any time by clearing your browser cache and site data.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact

For questions or feedback regarding this policy, please file an issue through the GitHub project repository.
